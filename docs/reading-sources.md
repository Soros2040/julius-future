# Reading sources and attribution

These materials are prompts for original reflection. The Bourdieu videos are a commentator's interpretation; quotations or theoretical claims about Bourdieu should be checked against an identified edition of his work. The stress video is a reading source, not medical evidence for this repository's personal essay.
